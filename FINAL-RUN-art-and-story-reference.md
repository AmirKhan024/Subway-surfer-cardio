# The Final Run — Art & Story Reference (optional companion to the re-skin prompt)

Richer detail for whoever builds the re-skin. The prompt is self-sufficient; this is here to make the world look and feel right rather than generic. Source: the "Isotropy — The Great Warrior Mission" design bible, Section 5. Everything here is theming/art guidance — none of it changes game logic.

## The one-line reason it exists
It begins in complete darkness and ends in the first daylight in the country. **The run is organised around that, not around any mechanic.** A soldier carries a treasury home, on foot, in the dark, and arrives on the plateau exactly as the sun clears the ridge. If the build nails only one thing, it's that dark-to-first-light arc.

## The land (for accurate scenery, not decoration)
- **Kaho** — India's first / easternmost *village*, ~1,240 m, on the bank of the Lohit. ~14–18 families, a primary school, one shop, a **gonpa (monastery)** roof and **prayer flags** that are the first thing you see coming off the ridge. Meyor (Zakhring) people, Mahayana Buddhist. The Line of Actual Control ridge is visible right above it.
- **The Lohit** — an easternmost tributary of the Brahmaputra. **Hard, cold, translucent green** (≈ `#1E7A5E`) — NOT the grey-brown of a plains river; getting the colour right matters. Where the valley opens, it **braids into 2–4 channels around long sandbars**, then rejoins. That braid is why the game has three lanes: channel / gravel bar / channel. Crossings are **swaying wire-rope-and-timber foot suspension bridges** and Bailey bridges.
- **Walong** — "place of bamboo groves"; approach is through **cane and bamboo dense enough to run under**. Site of the 1962 Battle of Walong; a war memorial on the Namti plains.
- **Dong** — a plateau where the Lohit meets the Sati stream; **where India's first sunrise is watched.** The finish line and the light source.

## What's growing / moving around you (scatter these)
Pine slopes thinning to scree and silver grass near the ridge · bamboo & cane groves · wild orchids on wet rock faces · rhododendron · terraced cardamom and millet on the slopes · **mithun** (big north-eastern bovine) grazing loose **with bells — the ambient sound of the dark** · hornbill and blood pheasant at dawn · **prayer flags on every ridge, bridge-head and gonpa roof** · **mani-stone** stacks at trail junctions.

## Colour — the whole level is one 60-second ramp
| Progress | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Start (dark) | `#10162E` | near-black indigo; almost monochrome; only prayer flags + lit windows are saturated |
| First light | `#2B3A4E` → `#4A5A66` | grey-blue, mist on the water |
| Saffron (~75%) | `#E8913A` | the sun's rim clears the eastern ridge |
| Gold | `#F5C542` | snow goes red then gold; the braided sandbars throw the light back so the valley floor "turns to metal" |
| Crest | near-white | first full light on the plateau |
| River accent | `#1E7A5E` | hard translucent green |

Restraint early is deliberate: keep Levels-of-brightness low at the start so the dawn has somewhere to climb to.

## Sound palette (procedural where you can; assets are a TODO)
- Ambient: **bansuri** (bamboo flute) melody over a low drone; a **cadence drum** that tracks stride rate (a slowing runner hears themselves slow).
- World: **mithun bells** panning in the dark, boots on scree, breath, the **river** as a constant bed, wire-rope bridge creak, bamboo knocking, first birds at dawn.
- Stings: **metal-on-metal** for a mohur (quiet, not a game chime); **lock-and-hinge** for a Sealed Kosha; the **dungchen (long horn)** for the biggest dawn moment. Keep Levels 1–2 restrained so the finish lands.

## Action vocabulary (story names for the existing actions — copy only, detection unchanged)
- **RUN** — high-knee march on the spot; cadence drives trail speed.
- **JUMP** — over fallen pine logs, stream channels, missing bridge planks, terrace walls.
- **SCOOP** — the squat; duck under cane/bamboo thickets, Bailey-bridge girders, low pine boughs, prayer-flag lines.
- **SHIFT** — lateral lane change across the braided channels; dodge boulders, scree fans, mid-stream rocks, switchbacks.

## Tone
Reverent and grounded. The setting is a real, sparsely populated frontier valley and a real 1962 battlefield — theme it with respect, no arcade gloss over the memorial, and let the sunrise do the emotional work.
